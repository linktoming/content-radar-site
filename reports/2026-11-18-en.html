🧠 Analysis

  • OpenAI on LinkedIn says Astra's per-task cost is under one-third of the previous generation. This is the vendor's claim, not a figure this report measured.
  • OpenAI: the roughly 2x efficiency in a study of 1,800+ researchers is self-reported. It is not a causal gain that applies to all work.
  • OpenAI: the physicist shared a candidate lattice definition of the Standard Model. The collaboration did not prove the Standard Model.
  • Harvey reports that Astra's full-pass rate on its legal benchmark is more than 3x the prior model's. Vertical gains may not match how general users experience the model.
