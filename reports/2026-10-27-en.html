🔭 Main Thread

  • Buy capabilities by task, not model prestige. OpenAI positions Terra around cheaper computer use; the useful metric is cost per successfully completed job.
  • Workflows are becoming portable assets. Open skills and recoverable agent execution make process, state and acceptance criteria more durable than isolated prompts.
  • Keep announcements separate from outcomes. Anthropic has filed IPO registration documents; that is not a completed listing.

🎯 Primary

  • OpenAI: GPT-6.1 Terra emphasizes lower-cost computer operation. An official video says standard API pricing is half the previous generation’s; availability includes specified paid plans and the API. Test your own long-running tasks before assuming the savings translate into cheaper delivery.
  • Anthropic: Agent Skills is moving to the Agentic AI Foundation, pointing toward cross-platform workflow reuse. Its IPO announcement concerns a registration filing that is not yet effective—not a completed IPO.
  • Google DeepMind: Gemini’s Blender MCP demonstration iterates from images into 3D scenes, extending creation toward editable spatial assets. Its Springer Nature collaboration explores peer-review assistance rather than replacing expert judgment.

💰 Investor

  • Watch workflow economics, not just token prices. OpenAI’s office-software benchmark claim puts Terra near Astra at a lower price; retries, supervision and completion rates still determine practical value.
  • Return to the filing source. Use Anthropic’s official announcement to distinguish confirmed facts from valuation commentary or optimistic forecasts.

🧠 Sense Maker

  • Automation needs boundaries. OpenAI describes offline training webpages and disconnected live internet, while its Brotli vulnerability report highlights risks below the prompt layer. Knowledge access and execution permissions deserve separate controls.
  • Look for work, not generic chat demand. OpenAI’s study spanning multiple countries and 165 work activities offers a task-level lens; Claude’s nonprofit skills apply that logic to fundraising, marketing and hiring.

🔨 Practitioner

  • Design for recovery: Vercel’s durable-harness focuses on resuming failed tasks. Store progress and define restart behavior.
  • Inspect skills like dependencies: NVIDIA’s SkillSpector provides security scanning for skill packages.
  • Close the diagnostic loop: OpenAI reports cutting incident investigation from hours to minutes with Codex Ultrafast. The transferable pattern is connecting evidence, diagnosis and execution.

🔥 Professional Radar

  • Independent checks: OpenAI invited METR and Redwood to assess internal monitoring.
  • Verification beyond generation: Anthropic is exploring Claude-assisted formal verification.
  • Reusable simulation assets: NVIDIA’s SimReady dataset combines scene objects with physical properties.

👥 My Feeds

  • Boris Cherny traces Agent Skills from a Claude Code experiment toward open governance: reusable processes may outlast client-specific features.
  • OpenAI’s dot demonstration connects feedback collection, issue tracking and Codex fixes—a useful loop for content products too.
