🔭 Main Thread

OpenAI cutting direct model access for Cursor turns AI coding tools into a supply-chain and platform-trust story, not just a product-feature race.

The news lane scanned 941 raw signals and produced 487 semantic candidates plus 350 trust-recalled items. The viral lane added 15 Chinese media candidates after browser preflight failed. The strongest crossover is the Cursor break: OpenAI's statement, Cursor's response, and a Chinese Jiqizhixin breaking-news item all point to the same anxiety: the AI workbench is becoming strategically dependent on upstream model politics.

For indie hackers and AI builders, the lesson is concrete. The asset is not the IDE, the prompt, or the model account; the asset is a portable workflow: logs, task specs, evaluation criteria, fallback models, reusable skills, and recovery paths. The same day also pushes the stack downward: Anthropic moves Claude into scientific workflows and hardware standards, while NVIDIA, OpenAI Jalapeno, and a16z point toward chips, memory, networking, power, and systems software as the next competitive layer.
